1. RETRIEVE THE INITIAL ADMINISTRATOR BOOTSTRAP PASSWORD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SkyOps generates a secure, one-time bootstrap credential upon first launch.
To retrieve the initial password, run:

  kubectl exec -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} deploy/{{ include "skyops.fullname" . }} -c skyops -- cat /app/.data/secrets/initial-admin-password

Or view it in the initial pod startup logs:

  kubectl logs -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} deploy/{{ include "skyops.fullname" . }} -c skyops | grep "INITIAL BOOTSTRAP PASSWORD" -A 4

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. ACCESS THE SKYOPS WEB CONSOLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Forward the SkyOps Control Plane service port to your local machine:

  kubectl port-forward -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} svc/{{ include "skyops.fullname" . }} 3000:{{ .Values.service.port }}

Then open your browser and navigate to:

  http://localhost:3000

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. COMPLETE FIRST-TIME ADMINISTRATOR SETUP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Open http://localhost:3000 in your browser.
  2. Enter the one-time bootstrap password retrieved in Step 1.
  3. Create your permanent administrator account with your corporate email and password.
  4. The temporary bootstrap credential will be permanently invalidated and purged.
  5. The SkyOps In-Cluster Agent will automatically discover and monitor this cluster!
